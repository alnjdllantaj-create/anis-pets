Pet Rabbit Care: Safe Housing and Daily Handling

✍️ The Anis Team📅 30 October 2026⏱ 2 min read

Rabbit care starts with a safe, calm environment and a suitable daily routine. Needs vary by age and circumstances; ask an experienced professional about appropriate food rather than relying on generic quantities online.

Prepare a safe space

  • Provide room to move and stretch, a hiding place and non-slip flooring.
  • Keep cables and harmful plants out of reach and provide a shaded, well-ventilated area.
  • Keep clean water available and clean the bowl and litter area regularly.
  • Provide suitable, safe things to chew and handle your rabbit calmly.

Everyday handling

Do not lift a rabbit by the ears or neck; support the chest and hindquarters together. An adult should supervise children and be responsible for care. Ask an experienced professional about grooming, nails and introducing a compatible companion.

If eating changes

If your rabbit stops eating or misses a meal, contact a veterinarian immediately, preferably one experienced with rabbits. Do not wait a fixed number of hours or substitute home treatment for the call.
