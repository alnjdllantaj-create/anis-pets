Our story

About us

Anis: a platform born in a house full of cats, and a dream of Arabic-language content worth trusting.

Why Anis?

The idea began with a simple question: why should an Arab pet owner bounce between machine-translated forums and contradictory content just to get one reliable answer? So we built the reference we always wished for: clear, practical and well organised.

Our editorial method

  • We simplify, never dumb down:We explain the reasoning behind every tip, to build understanding rather than dependence.
  • We update what changes:Every article carries its last-review date; outdated facts are corrected, never silently deleted.
  • We know our limits:We are not a substitute for your veterinarian, and we say so plainly in every article.
  • No copied content:Every article is originally written by the Anis team or reviewed line by line.

Our team

Writers and editors who are lifelong pet lovers, reviewed before publication by collaborating veterinary professionals, and led by an editor with over a decade of hands-on experience with cats, dogs and parrots.
