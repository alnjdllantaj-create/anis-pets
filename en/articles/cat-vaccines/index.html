Cat Vaccinations: Questions to Ask Your Veterinarian

✍️ The Anis Team📅 30 October 2026⏱ 2 min read

Vaccination needs and recommendations vary with a cat's age, circumstances and location. This page provides no schedule or doses; discuss an appropriate plan with your veterinarian.

Questions to ask at the appointment

  • Which vaccines are appropriate for my cat, and why?
  • How should I keep the vaccination record and appointments?
  • What should I do if an appointment is missed or a problem appears after the visit?
  • How can I contact the clinic outside its regular hours if needed?

Keep your cat's record and share it with the clinic. A general schedule found online is not an individual plan.

Further reading: WSAVA Vaccination Guidelines (PDF). This page is general information, not a prescription.
