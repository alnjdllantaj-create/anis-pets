My Cat Won't Eat: When to Contact a Veterinarian

✍️ The Anis Team📅 30 October 2026⏱ 2 min read
Ad space — paste your Google AdSense unit code here once your account is approved

If your cat stops eating or its appetite changes markedly, contact a veterinarian as soon as you notice. Do not wait a full day to ask for advice.

What information can you share?

  • When your cat last ate and whether they are drinking as usual.
  • Any changes you have noticed in behaviour or activity.
  • Any medicines already being taken and recent changes in food or surroundings.

Do not diagnose the cause yourself

An article or a single sign cannot determine the cause. Do not give medication or force your cat to eat without veterinary direction. If your cat appears to be in an emergency, seek urgent veterinary care.

Further reading: Cornell Feline Health Center: anorexia. This is general information, not a diagnosis or treatment.
