Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. Visiting the site

Anis is an informational website hosted on Netlify. When pages load, the hosting provider handles technical requests that may include an IP address, browser information and time, under its own policies. See Netlify's privacy policy.

2. Language preference

Your choice may be saved in your browser's local storage as anis-lang for a later visit. You can clear it in your browser's site-data settings. If storage is disabled, the site remains usable without remembering the choice.

3. Contact

The contact form opens a draft in your email application; the website does not automatically send the message. It is sent only if you review and send it. Your email provider and the recipient's provider then process the message; do not include sensitive information you do not wish to share.

4. External services and links

Fonts are hosted with the site files. The current site files do not contain active Google Analytics or AdSense scripts. Source and sharing links open external services when clicked; those services have their own policies.

5. Questions and changes

For a question or to request correction or deletion of a message you sent, use the contact page. This page will be updated if services or site behaviour change; your rights may depend on applicable law.
